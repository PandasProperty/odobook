Odobook

The fuel and maintenance log that stays on your phone. For cars, trucks, motorcycles, RVs and boats. No account, no ads.

Download on the App Store Support Coming soon to Google Play.

What it does

Bring your history

Imports files exported from Fuelly (website and iPhone app) and aCar backups. Preview everything, fix units and dates, then save.

Fuel and MPG

Full-tank MPG, cost per mile and spending, with partial and missed fill-ups handled the right way.

Service log and reminders

Log oil changes, tires, brakes and more. Set reminders by miles, months or both, and get a notification when one is due.

Yours to keep

Your log lives on your phone. Export it as CSV or save a backup file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive whenever you like.

Any vehicle, any units

Miles, kilometers or engine hours; gallons or liters. Each vehicle keeps its own.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Read the privacy policy.
